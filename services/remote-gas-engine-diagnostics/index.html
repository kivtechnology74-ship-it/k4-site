Gas engine down? Find the fault before replacing expensive components.

Independent remote fault isolation for gas-engine power plants.

When a gas engine stops, multiple alarms do not necessarily mean multiple failed components. K4 helps site teams work from symptoms and measurements toward a narrower, evidence-based fault location before unnecessary parts are replaced or specialist mobilisation is arranged.

Remote initial diagnostics

When Remote Diagnostics Makes Sense

  • Engine will not start
  • Repeated shutdowns or unexplained trips
  • Controller communication faults
  • Multiple simultaneous alarms
  • Ignition-system faults
  • Abnormal 24 VDC control circuits
  • Suspected ECU or controller failure
  • Intermittent electrical faults
  • Commissioning problems
  • OEM engineer unavailable or difficult to mobilise
  • Uncertainty over whether an expensive electronic module actually requires replacement

The purpose is not to guess the failed component. The purpose is to reduce the fault area systematically using available evidence.

Structured fault isolation

How it works

01 / SEND THE EVIDENCE

Start with what is available

Alarm screenshots, engine manufacturer and model, controller / ECU type, cabinet photographs, relays / fuses / terminals, electrical drawings, operating state when the fault occurred and recent maintenance or intervention history.

Incomplete documentation does not automatically prevent an initial review.

02 / BUILD THE FAULT MAP

Separate evidence from assumptions

K4 separates confirmed facts, reported symptoms, independent and common circuits, possible common-mode failures, assumptions and unknown points requiring verification.

03 / CHECK THE HIGHEST-VALUE POINTS

Focus the next measurement

The site technician receives focused checks rather than a generic troubleshooting list. Depending on configuration and site procedures, these may include visual inspection, fuse verification, connector status, control-voltage measurements, relay-state checks, supply-path verification or controller status.

05 / ENGINEER REVIEW

Engineering responsibility stays with people

AI can help organise evidence, compare alarms and maintain the diagnostic tree. It does not replace engineering responsibility.

Actions involving energised equipment, protection systems, ignition circuits, safety interlocks or other elevated-risk conditions require appropriate site procedures and qualified personnel.
