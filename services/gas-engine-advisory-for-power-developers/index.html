The owner decision

Build the project basis before a vendor defines it for you.

K4 works as an external gas-engine competence layer for teams that can develop and finance power projects but need deeper, independent expertise on reciprocating-engine technology, equipment evidence and lifecycle exposure.

01 / PLATFORM

Select the engine platform

Compare duty fit, fuel compatibility, output, efficiency, maintainability, service support and project constraints without declaring a universal winner.

02 / FLEET

Size the fleet architecture

Test unit size, number of units, dependable net capacity, maintenance states and the consequence of losing one or more units.

03 / EQUIPMENT

Choose new, used or rebuilt

Balance lead time and time-to-power against condition evidence, remaining lifecycle, restoration scope and commercial risk.

04 / ECONOMICS

Model the lifecycle

Connect fuel, maintenance intervals, major overhauls, reserves, auxiliary loads and replacement assumptions to the investment case.

05 / RELIABILITY

Challenge the reliability basis

Review redundancy, scheduled outages, forced events, common-mode exposure, critical spares and service-response assumptions.

06 / OWNER SIDE

Control technical decisions

Define requirements, normalize bids, identify data-room gaps and establish evidence and acceptance gates before award.

K4 advisory framework

SELECT → VERIFY → OPERATE

The framework follows the decisions a project must make, not the catalogue structure of an equipment supplier.

SELECT01

Choose the project basis.

  • Architecture and operating duty
  • Platform and fuel fit
  • Unit and fleet sizing
  • New versus pre-owned
  • Lifecycle economics
VERIFY02

Test claims against evidence.

  • Supplier scope and assumptions
  • Serial and configuration
  • Condition and lifecycle history
  • FAT and inspection scope
  • Technical due diligence
OPERATE03

Prepare for real operating states.

  • Maintenance and overhaul rotation
  • Redundancy and common-mode risk
  • Critical spares and service response
  • Availability methodology
  • Operating-data review
