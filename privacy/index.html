Privacy Notice

Effective date: 21 November 2026

KIV-Technology d.o.o. (“K4”, “we”) operates k4-technology.com. Privacy requests: ceo@k4-technology.com.

Data handling

Cloudflare provides website delivery, security, Workers and D1. Cloudflare may process network/device data, including IP addresses, to deliver and protect the site. The calculator analytics endpoint uses the connecting IP temporarily for rate limiting; K4 calculator event rows do not store IP addresses.

Analytics

Analytics is optional. Google Analytics 4 (GA4) is not loaded and calculator analytics events are not sent to GA4 or D1 until you choose “Accept analytics”. After acceptance, GA4 receives page/campaign context, browser/device information and site interactions. K4 calculator events include event type, language, calculator version, GPU brand/model ID/model name, profile category and opaque session/calculation IDs. They exclude project/site names, project financial or engineering inputs, form contents and contact details. The choice is stored in a first-party cookie for six months and can be changed with “Privacy settings” in the footer.

Forms

When you submit a contact or diagnostic form, the information you enter (such as contact details, message and selected technical details), language, campaign source and time zone are stored in Cloudflare D1 so K4 can respond. A copy is sent to the K4 team through its configured email relay. Refusing analytics does not restrict the calculator, PDF printing or forms. A request is linked to a calculator record only when analytics consent exists and the request has been stored successfully.

Retention and choices

K4 retains enquiry records while needed to respond and maintain business records. D1 calculator event rows are used for measurement/reporting; automatic expiry is not currently configured. GA4 retention is controlled by the K4 property settings. You may refuse or withdraw analytics consent at any time using the footer control. For access, correction or deletion requests, contact ceo@k4-technology.com. Changing analytics consent does not delete a form already submitted.
