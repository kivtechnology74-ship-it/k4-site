Used equipment / asset and fleet DD

A running engine is not automatically a suitable fleet asset.

K4 tests whether the exact machine — and the fleet around it — can support the intended output and duty over its remaining lifecycle.

  1. 01Exact identity and configurationSerial-specific evidence across engine, generator, controls and BOP.
  2. 02Operating and lifecycle historyHours, controller records, repairs, replacements and overhaul events reconciled.
  3. 03Condition and preservationBorescope, corrosion, storage, leaks, fluids and physical evidence.
  4. 04FAT and load-test evidenceAcceptance criteria, stable load, alarms, trips and auxiliary performance.
  5. 05Project-duty fitFuel, electrical configuration, remaining lifecycle and dependable fleet output.
  6. 06Transaction consequenceBUY, BUY WITH CONDITIONS, PRICE ADJUSTMENT or DO NOT BUY.
Decision boundary: conclusions are limited by the evidence available. Unresolved items remain visible rather than being converted into unsupported guarantees.

Clear answers

What clients and technical reviewers ask first.

What decisions can K4-Technology support?

Which gas-engine platform fits the duty, whether new or pre-owned equipment is rational, how lifecycle economics compare, what reliability architecture is required and which supplier claims need verification before capital is committed.

Does K4-Technology act as the EPC contractor?

Our default model is client-side advisory. The client contracts a qualified local execution contractor directly; we define requirements, evaluate bids and verify delivery. Any broader scope is agreed separately.

What inputs are needed for a rapid project screen?

Useful starting inputs are a gas analysis, site and load data, utility constraints, supplier quotations and any available equipment or project documents.

What does pre-owned genset due diligence deliver?

A written engineering report with photo and video evidence, condition findings, residual-life assessment, restoration budget and a fair-market-value opinion.
