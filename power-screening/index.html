Limitations

This simplified annual model treats load using a single utilization factor. It does not confirm that a grid offer is available. Gas generation is unavailable without an applicable confirmed OEM curve and project gas price with units and LHV/HHV basis. Solar, BESS and hybrid dispatch are outside this version.
